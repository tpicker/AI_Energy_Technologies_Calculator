Interpretation matters: Generation, standalone battery storage power, and Solar + Storage capacity are reported separately. Battery storage shifts electricity in time; it does not generate electricity. MW describes storage power, not energy duration (MWh).

The workbook gives one capacity range for Solar + Storage without a generation/storage split. That range is excluded from both generation and standalone storage totals to avoid double counting; its full cost and construction time are included.

Nameplate MW does not establish firm, continuously available supply. Consider reliability, emissions, siting, permitting, and local conditions before making a recommendation.

How should I interpret the results?
  • Compare the selected portfolio's range against the AI scale you are studying.
  • Ask whether capacity is dispatchable or intermittent and whether the location can support the technology.
  • Cost ranges represent approximate total project capital costs from the supplied course workbook, not lifetime costs.
  • Each unit repeats the workbook's representative project range; quantities multiply both ends of its capacity and capital-cost ranges.
  • Parallel construction assumes all selected units can start together and uses the longest minimum and maximum build time. Sequential construction assumes no overlap and adds the build time for every unit. Both are simplified planning scenarios.
  • Storage and unsplit Solar + Storage capacity are shown separately from generation. Storage energy capacity and duration are not supplied by the workbook.
